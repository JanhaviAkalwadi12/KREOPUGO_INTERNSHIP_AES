Kindergarten

A warm and engaging start to a child’s school journey.

Learning Through Curiosity & Play

Kindergarten learning is designed to help young children feel comfortable, curious and ready to explore. Classroom experiences combine stories, play, creative work, conversation, movement and simple hands-on activities.

LanguageStories, conversation, vocabulary and early reading.
NumeracyCounting, patterns, shapes and everyday number activities.
CreativityDrawing, craft, music and imaginative expression.
Social SkillsSharing, participation, routines and positive interaction.
