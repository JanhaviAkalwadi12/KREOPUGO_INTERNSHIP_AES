Kindergarten Syllabus

A foundational programme designed to build early learning habits.

This is a school resource page for practice/reference. Please follow the latest official school communication for the current syllabus or assessment schedule.

Language & Communication

Listening, speaking, vocabulary, early literacy and story-based learning.

Numeracy

Counting, number sense, shapes, patterns and simple mathematical thinking.

Creative & Physical Development

Art, music, movement, play and social-emotional development.
